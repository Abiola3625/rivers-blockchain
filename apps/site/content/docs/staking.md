# Staking

Staking locks RVRB with a validator. It gives the validator voting power and gives you a share of its rewards.

## Stake

1. Open **Stake** in Rivers Wallet.
2. Pick a validator. The list shows each validator's stake and commission.
3. Enter an amount and confirm.

Your stake becomes active at the start of the next epoch (epochs last 24 hours).

## Rewards

At the end of each epoch, the protocol pays rewards to validators in proportion to their stake. Each validator keeps its commission from its delegators' share; the rest is added to your stake. The Stake page shows your projected rewards at the current rate.

## Unstake

Choose **Unstake** on a stake. The RVRB and its rewards return to your wallet at the end of the epoch.

## Choosing a validator

- Check the validator in Rivers Scan: a validator that misses checkpoints helps the network less.
- Compare commissions.
- Spread stake across validators to help keep the network decentralized. No validator can hold more than 10% of the voting power, however much stake it receives.
