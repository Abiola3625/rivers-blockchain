# Risks and legal notice

## Technology risk

Rivers is new software. Despite testing and reviews, the chain, the packages, the websites and the bridge may contain errors that lead to loss of funds. The testnet may be reset at any time.

## Cryptography risk

Shielded payments rely on a trusted setup. The testnet setup has one contributor and offers no protection against a dishonest contributor. The security of Groth16, Poseidon and BN254 rests on assumptions that are widely studied but not proven.

## Market risk

The price of RVRB and of any token on Rivers can fall sharply or to zero. Lending positions can be liquidated when prices move. Liquidity providers can lose value when prices change.

## Bridge risk

Bridges hold large amounts of value and are a frequent target of attacks. A failure of the bridge contracts, the bridge nodes or the committee could lead to loss of bridged assets.

## Regulatory risk

Laws on digital assets in Nigeria and elsewhere are changing. Some features or tokens may become restricted in some places. Users are responsible for following the laws that apply to them.
