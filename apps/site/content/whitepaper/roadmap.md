# Roadmap

The roadmap lists what is running, what is being built and what must happen before mainnet. It gives no dates; items move when they are ready and reviewed.

## Running on the testnet

- The Rivers chain: RVRB with the fixed supply, the fifty-year reward schedule, daily epochs and open validator entry
- Rivers Wallet, Rivers Scan, Rivers Connect and Rivers Validator, each as its own website
- The Rivers indexer and its public API
- Token Studio, with optional team vesting and a starting pool
- Investor and team vesting
- Rivers DEX with time-weighted prices
- Rivers Lend
- Shielded payments for RVRB and USDs
- USDs and NGNs test stablecoins

## In development

- Bridge to Ethereum, starting with Sepolia
- Private sends submitted by a relayer, so the fee payer is not linked
- The testnet on public servers, so outside validators can join

## Before mainnet

- External security audit of the chain changes and every Rivers package
- Fixes for the open findings of the internal review
- A public trusted setup ceremony for shielded payments
- Genesis wallets created by their owners on their own devices
- Administrator capabilities moved to multi-signature wallets
- Licensed issuers for naira and dollar stablecoins
