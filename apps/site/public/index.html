Every block, settled by many hands

Rivers runs the Sui engine: Move smart contracts, an object model that lets unrelated payments execute side by side, and Mysticeti consensus among a committee of staked validators. No single company can rewrite the ledger.

Twenty-one million RVRB, never more

Ten million RVRB exist from day one. The other eleven million are paid to stakers, a little each day, for fifty years. Every five years the daily reward halves, so new supply slows as the network grows.

Built at home, open to the world

Rivers is designed and built in Nigeria. Naira and dollar stablecoins sit next to RVRB in every wallet. Banks and payment firms join Rivers Connect, send ISO 20022 payments to each other, and settle on one shared ledger instead of waiting on batch files.

  • NGNs and USDs test stablecoins with a built-in exchange
  • Per-institution limits, suspension and an on-chain audit trail
  • Prices in naira across the wallet and the explorer

Five doors into one network

Each product is its own site with its own address. They read the same chain, so a payment sent in the wallet shows in Scan a moment later.

Rivers Wallet

Your keys stay on your device. Hold RVRB and stablecoins, swap, stake with a validator, lend and borrow, and claim vested tokens.

  • Twelve recovery words, encrypted with your password
  • Swap, pools and lending in one place
  • Locks itself after fifteen minutes

Rivers Scan

Watch every transaction land in real time. Look up any address, token, validator or pool, and launch your own token in Token Studio.

  • Live transaction view
  • Holders, rich list and daily charts
  • Token Studio with vesting and a starting pool

Rivers Connect

For banks and payment firms. Pay another member in naira stablecoin, upload a batch file, and export pacs.008 messages for your core banking system.

  • Single and batch payments
  • Liquidity conversion
  • Compliance view and developer keys

Rivers Validator

Run a node, join the committee and earn a share of each day's rewards. Set your commission and see your delegators.

  • One-command server setup
  • Rewards and signing record
  • Join, update and leave

Rivers Core

The control room for the Rivers foundation. Every action is signed on chain by the administrator key, so the record is public.

  • Health, alerts and invariant checks
  • Admit and limit institutions
  • Investor vesting and market pauses
